Policy FAQ

Key questions about deposits, withdrawals, refunds and personal data.

文書バージョン 2026-09-30.1, 施行日 2026-09-30

Can I deposit real funds now?

Payment, withdrawal and delivery availability is shown on the relevant screen. Unconnected services are unavailable; unprocessed requests are never shown as completed. See Business and service information for operator details and supported regions.

Is 100% resale the same as a refund?

No. Optional resale based on item valuation is distinct from cancellation, defects and unused-fund return. Statutory refunds must not automatically incur a 5% deduction.

Must I buy more to recover funds?

Additional purchasing is not an approved condition for returning unused deposits or statutory refunds. The existing purchase-usage screen is under review and is not described as a legal AML requirement.

Does a deposit timer erase my funds?

A timer is not a funds-forfeiture rule. Real transfers require chain-record investigation; automatic credit within 72 hours is not guaranteed. Do not send real funds to the current interface.

What are the fees and processing times?

The withdrawal screen shows an ordinary minimum of 20 USDT and fees of 1.00 USDT for TRC-20 and 0.50 USDT for BEP-20. Check service availability and final conditions when requesting. Instant transfer or completion within 24 hours is not guaranteed, and ordinary minimums do not extinguish statutory refunds or remaining-funds claims on closure.

How do I delete data or make a request?

Request account closure from your profile after checking balances, held goods and pending transactions and resolving them as needed. Actual account deletion cannot complete without the authentication service connection. Deleting browser site data removes local records, not a server account or legally retained records. Logging out is not complete deletion either. Check operating information for the official privacy-rights contact; unconfirmed intake channels are unavailable.
