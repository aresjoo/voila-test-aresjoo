Deposits & payments

Supported networks, mistaken transfers and balance-credit rules.

Document version 2026-09-30.1, Date wey e start 2026-09-30

Verify the official deposit route

Send USDT only to the dedicated address issued in your account deposit screen. TRC-20, BEP-20 and ERC-20 are supported. The minimum deposit is 1 USDT on BEP-20, 1 USDT on ERC-20 and 12 USDT on TRC-20. Never transfer to an address sent in a message or by an impersonating account.

Networks and deposit confirmation

USDT deposits are supported on TRC-20, BEP-20 and ERC-20. Confirm that the network and address match before transfer, and check the amount after sender fees and any required memo.

Confirmation, crediting and timers

A blockchain deposit is credited once the transaction, recipient address, asset, amount and confirmation status match. A session timer never affects the asset; delays, reorganizations and address changes are shown in deposit status and handled through transaction review.

Mistaken, small, duplicate or excess transfers

Transfers on the wrong network, token or address, with a missing memo, below the minimum, duplicated or in excess are checked case by case using the transaction ID and proof of ownership. Even when recovery is difficult, we look for a workable route with you. We tell you feasibility, timing and actual costs first, and never charge a fee without your consent or ask for another deposit as a condition.

On-chain deposits and transaction records

A deposit is credited once the blockchain transaction, recipient address, asset, amount and confirmation status match. Transaction history displays the network, amount, timestamp and TxID.

Item handling and cashback

A winning item may be delivered free after authentication, or converted to 100% instant cashback if you do not want it.

Conversion and asset risk

USDT is not fiat money or a bank deposit and carries depegging, issuer-freeze and network risks. Indicative conversions do not guarantee execution or refund rates. Quote validity, rounding and fees are shown before you order, and no new charge is added to a transaction you already authorized.
