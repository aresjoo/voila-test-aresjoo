    Good to know

    Every open adds 10% of the box price to the burst gauge. When Burst comes up (odds 0.625%) you receive the whole gauge in USDT, and it restarts from $200. The odds of Burst are always the same, however full the gauge is. The gauge belongs to this box and this account, and is paid only when it bursts.

    Expected value is the odds-weighted average you would receive, with every result taken as USDT, divided by the box price. It is an average over many opens and does not guarantee a single result. An item taken as USDT pays 100% of its item value.
