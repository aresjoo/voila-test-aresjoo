    Good to know

    Every open adds 10% of the box price to the burst gauge. When Burst comes up (odds 0.625%) you receive the whole gauge in USDT, and it restarts from $50. The odds of Burst are always the same, however full the gauge is. The gauge belongs to this box and this account, and is paid only when it bursts.

    The expected value ratio is the sum of the probability-weighted average item value and the share added to the burst gauge, divided by the box price. It is an average over many openings and does not guarantee any single result.
