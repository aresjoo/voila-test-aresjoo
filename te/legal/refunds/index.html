Cancellation & refunds

Separate remedies for unopened orders, delivered results, defects and unused funds.

Statutory refunds differ from resale

Choosing to resell an inventory item at a stated percentage does not replace statutory cancellation or defect remedies. Do not automatically deduct 5% for valid cancellation/non-performance or force repayment only in points or USDT. Applicable law determines refund scope, method and timing, while more favorable contractual rights remain.

Withdrawal rights in Korean consumer transactions

Where Article 17 of the Korean E-Commerce Act applies, the ordinary period is seven days after receipt of written contract information, with statutory rules moving the start to later supply or commencement where applicable. Nonconformity with advertising or contract engages the separate three-month-from-supply and thirty-day-from-awareness rules. Apply the statutory starting points and exceptions rather than treating one period as universal across countries and products.

Before/after opening and digital delivery

Paid orders without commenced results, duplicate charges and erroneous debits need cancellation/correction after verification. Clicking a button does not remove all statutory rights. Any restriction following digital supply requires the correct legal classification and all applicable conditions, such as advance notice, separate consent or trial access. The treatment of chance-based products needs specific pre-launch review.

Defects, wrong delivery and non-supply

Defects, counterfeits, misdescription, transit damage/loss, missing delivery and stock failure are distinct from a change of mind. Verify with order/product information, photos or reasonable alternatives and provide applicable replacement, redelivery, reduction, cancellation or refund. Lack of an unboxing video or use of a different contact route must not automatically defeat statutory rights.

Return costs and refund timing

Distinguish change-of-mind return costs from seller-fault costs; do not add arbitrary cancellation penalties. Where Article 18 of the Korean E-Commerce Act applies, follow the three-business-day refund rule from the applicable trigger, including goods returned or withdrawal for services/digital content or unsupplied goods, and applicable delay compensation. Distinguish initiating a card refund from the issuer posting it.

Request details and original-payment return

The request channel will be published when official support opens. Basic details are order/transaction ID, an identity-verification method, the requested remedy/reason and necessary evidence, never full card numbers or private keys. Cash, card, crypto and mixed payments require source-aware return routes and explanations of any relevant conversion or fees. Problems with the original sale must not be diverted into resale.

Refusals and appeals

A refusal based on an exception, use or consumer fault needs legal and factual grounds and a review route. Internal review cannot arbitrarily shorten legal claim periods or become the only allowed remedy. Reconcile duplicate goods/refund issues from records without arbitrarily deducting unrelated legitimate balances.

Canceling before confirmation and closing an opening

At the quantity and total review step, Cancel purchase stops the order without a charge. After confirmation, the entire selected quantity is processed together and all results are recorded. X or SKIP bypasses the animation to show complete results; it is not a cancellation or refund request. Cashback goes to the balance and other goods to the vault. Stopping autoplay prevents the next purchase and preserves the current purchase results. Duplicate charges, missing results, non-performance and statutory cancellation or refund rights remain subject to separate review and remedies.

Canceling a delivery request before dispatch

A delivery request in preparation can be canceled and the item returned to held inventory. Any shipping fee already charged is returned once, limited to the amount allocated to that item and following its original funding sources. Shared shipping fees follow per-item allocation records without duplicate reimbursement. A request cannot be canceled through the screen while in transit. This operational restriction does not remove statutory withdrawal, return or remedies for defects, wrong delivery, loss or non-performance; those requests follow a separate return or refund review.
