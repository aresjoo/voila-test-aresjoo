Product types and fulfillment

Physical goods, digital codes and balance results have different fulfillment methods. Disclose exact model/specification/condition/contents, regional restrictions, delivery eligibility and digital expiry before ordering. Collection photography does not establish the delivered SKU. Vehicles and high-value regulated items require registration, insurance, age/import rules and responsible parties to be settled before sale.

Shipping, taxes and regional restrictions

Review the shipping fee applicable to the order on the delivery request screen. Orders marked free shipping have a zero shipping fee; this does not waive customs duties or taxes. Actual eligible destinations and contracted carriers require separate confirmation. Before ordering, disclose the origin, eligible destinations, freight and remote-area charges, tax allocation and import conditions as part of the total cost. Do not add charges without assent or unilaterally replace unavailable goods. You are responsible for all applicable taxes, including customs duties. Delivery takes an average of six months and may take longer. If additional information is needed, we will contact you at your registered email address.

Requests, dispatch and tracking

Provide a chance to correct recipient and item details before requesting delivery. Post-dispatch address changes depend on actual carrier conditions and costs, with assistance where possible. No 24-hour dispatch or specific carrier label is guaranteed before contracts and stock are confirmed. Show only genuine tracking numbers and explain delays, estimated timing and cancellation/refund options.

Returns, loss, damage and authenticity

Seller fault, loss, damage and suspected counterfeits require investigation and applicable remedies under the refund policy. An inspection body and enhanced compensation terms are unconfirmed, so 300% compensation is not an established operating promise. This policy does not retroactively diminish valid existing commitments or statutory rights. Publish the return address, method and cost allocation before actual sales.

Resale values and confirming a choice

Optional resale applies 100% to the displayed valuation of physical or digital items; balance results are automatically credited at their full displayed amount. This is not a 100% refund of the purchase price or principal protection. Disclose the valuation basis and time, condition, currency and actual resale amount before confirmation. Delivery and resale cannot proceed simultaneously for the same item; separate error, non-performance and statutory remedies remain available.

Digital codes, non-receipt and storage

Digital codes need territory/device/expiry details and investigation procedures for invalid or previously used codes, with withdrawal conditions disclosed before release. Long-unclaimed or undeliverable goods require reasonable contact and alternatives, not automatic forfeiture after an arbitrary short deadline. Any storage duration or fee must be disclosed before purchase and respect statutory rights.
