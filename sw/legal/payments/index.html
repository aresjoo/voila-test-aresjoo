Deposits & payments

Supported networks, mistaken transfers and balance-credit rules.

Toleo la hati 2026-09-30.1, Tarehe ya kuanza kutumika 2026-09-30

Verify the official deposit route

Send USDT only to the dedicated address issued in your account deposit screen. TRC-20, BEP-20 and ERC-20 are supported, with a minimum deposit of {minDeposit} USDT. Never transfer to an address sent in a message or by an impersonating account.

Networks and deposit confirmation

USDT deposits are supported on TRC-20, BEP-20 and ERC-20. Confirm that the network and address match before transfer, and check the amount after sender fees and any required memo.

Confirmation, crediting and timers

A blockchain deposit is credited once the transaction, recipient address, asset, amount and confirmation status match. A session timer never affects the asset; delays, reorganizations and address changes are shown in deposit status and handled through transaction review.

Mistaken, small, duplicate or excess transfers

Wrong networks, tokens, addresses, missing memos, below-minimum, duplicate and excess transfers require individual investigation using transaction IDs and ownership evidence. Recovery may be technically impossible, but responsibility is not automatically assigned entirely to the user. Explain feasibility, timing and actual costs first; no arbitrary recovery fee or additional deposit may be imposed as a recovery condition.

On-chain deposits and transaction records

A deposit is credited once the blockchain transaction, recipient address, asset, amount and confirmation status match. Transaction history displays the network, amount, timestamp and TxID.

Item handling and cashback

A winning item may be delivered free after authentication, or converted to 100% instant cashback if you do not want it.

Mistaken and duplicate deposits

Wrong networks, tokens or addresses, missing memos, duplicate transfers and excess deposits are reviewed using the transaction ID and ownership evidence. Recovery feasibility, timing and actual costs are explained, and no additional deposit is required for recovery.

Conversion and asset risk

USDT is not fiat money or a bank deposit and carries depegging, issuer-freeze and network risks. Indicative conversions do not guarantee execution or refund rates. Quote validity, rounding and fees must be disclosed before ordering; newly introduced charges must not be retroactively applied to authorized transactions.
