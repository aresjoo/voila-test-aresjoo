Withdrawals & unused funds

Limits, fees, reviews, wrong transfers and remedies for blocked withdrawals.

Bersyon ng dokumento 2026-09-30.1, Petsa ng bisa 2026-09-30

Withdrawals versus refunds

External withdrawal of a product-related balance, unused-funds return and statutory refunds are separate processes. Card funds follow the original-payment cancellation or refund route. Users are not required to purchase additional boxes to recover unused funds or statutory refunds. Purchase-usage figures or internal risk scores do not replace legal obligations or extinguish return rights.

Networks, minimums and costs

The withdrawal screen shows a minimum ordinary withdrawal of {minWithdraw} USDT, with fees of {trcFee} USDT for TRC-20 and {bepFee} USDT for BEP-20. Review the net amount after fees before requesting. ERC-20 withdrawal is not offered. The withdrawal connection and operating conditions must be confirmed; ordinary minimums alone cannot defeat statutory refunds or remaining-funds claims on account closure.

Address checks and broadcast

Users must be able to verify recipient network, asset, address and any memo. Format validation does not prove ownership or receivability. Provide final confirmation and explain when cancellation ceases to be possible. A broadcast may be irreversible; an explorer link must only be shown for an actual transaction ID.

Identity checks and further review

Affected transactions may be restricted as necessary while checking account compromise, reversals, sanctions, funding sources or lawful requests. Unless disclosure is legally prohibited, explain the reason, necessary evidence, estimated review time, status and appeal route. A risk score is a review signal, not proof of wrongdoing. Provide explanations and human review of automated decisions where applicable law requires them.

Delays, failures and balance restoration

Distinguish received, under review, broadcast and completed states; approval within 24 hours or instant transfer is not guaranteed. Operations must explain delays and the next update time. On failure or cancellation, reconcile the chain before releasing or returning reserved funds without duplicate payment, and define fees so non-incurred network costs are not charged.

Unused funds, small balances and closure

A return-request route is needed even below normal withdrawal minimums, during restrictions or at service closure. After identity and funding checks, explain an allowed original-payment or alternative return method, actual costs and deadline. Do not require another deposit, a separate advance tax transfer or another product purchase to release funds.

Appealing a restriction

The dispute route should accept transaction ID, time, amount, network and relevant notices. Never request private keys, recovery phrases or full card details. Lawful preservation or freezing orders require legally appropriate scope and duration; restricted funds are not operator revenue. The formal intake channel has not yet opened.
